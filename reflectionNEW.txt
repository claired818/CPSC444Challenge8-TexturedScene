1. What object did you make interactive using raycasting?
    I chose to make the stacks of coins interactive using raycasting.
2. How does raycasting differ from collision detection?
    Collision detection is used to see if objects are touching. Raycasting is used to see if one object is facing the other.
3. Why must a ray have both an origin and a direction?
    Because a ray only goes in one direction, unlike a collision box that goes in all directions.
4. What sound effect did you choose and why?
    I chose the sound of coins hitting together to go with my coin pickup interaction.
5. How did adding sound and interaction improve your scene?
    It made the scene feel much less stagnant.
